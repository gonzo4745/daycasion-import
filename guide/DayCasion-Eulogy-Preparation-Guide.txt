DAYCASION EULOGY PREPARATION GUIDE
For “My Eulogy Information (AI-assisted)”
==============================================================================

This guide helps you tell DayCasion what you would like said about your life.
When your Legacy process begins, DayCasion uses what you write here, with AI,
to prepare your eulogy. Your Legacy Executor receives only the finished
eulogy, never this guide, your answers or your notes.

How to use this guide
- Every question is optional. Skip any of them, answer in any order, and write
  as much or as little as you like.
- The questions are only suggestions. You can write about anything, and the
  last section, Additional Information You Want Included, has room for
  everything else.
- Write in your own words below each question. There is no length limit: in
  the Word version the answer boxes grow as you type, and in the text version
  you can add as many lines as you need.
- Please keep the question headings as they are. DayCasion uses them to find
  the tone you want (question 10) and the subjects you would prefer to leave
  out (question 9).
- Include real names, places and dates for anything you want mentioned.
  DayCasion never invents details you have not given.
- If you want your exact words read, such as a message to loved ones, put them
  in quotation marks.

When you are finished
- Save the finished guide as a Word document (.docx), PDF or plain text file
  (.txt).
- Go to import.daycasion.com, sign in, choose your My Life profile, then
  Import My Eulogy > My Eulogy Information (AI-assisted).
- Files can be up to 10 MB with up to about 150,000 characters of writing. The
  text must be typed: scanned or handwritten pages can’t be read.
- You can upload an updated version at any time until your Legacy process
  begins.

Already written a finished eulogy that should be read exactly as you wrote it?
Don’t use this guide. Choose My Completed Eulogy (No AI) instead: it is
delivered word for word and is never sent to AI.
